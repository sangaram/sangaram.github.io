I'm a PhD candidate at [CEA-List](https://list.cea.fr/en/) and [Paris-Saclay university](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en). My research is focused on video generation. While current generative models achieve impressive results by only relying on textual descriptions, natural language provides a limited control on the composition of the scene. Hence, I'm particarly interested in developing new methods for bringing more control on the spatio-temporal aspects of the generated videos. Apart from that, I have a broader interest in unsupervised representation learning.

Prior to that I did an MSc Eng in Computer Science with a major in artificial intelligence at [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/en/home), [Institut Polytechnique de Paris](https://www.ip-paris.fr/en).
