Amadou S. Sangare

PhD candidate at CEA-List and Paris-Saclay university

I’m a PhD candidate at CEA-List and Paris-Saclay university. My research is focused on video generation. While current generative models achieve impressive results by only relying on textual descriptions, natural language provides a limited control on the composition of the scene. Hence, I’m particarly interested in developing new methods for bringing more control on the spatio-temporal aspects of the generated videos. Apart from that, I have a broader interest in unsupervised representation learning.

Prior to that I did an MSc Eng in Computer Science with a major in artificial intelligence at Télécom Paris, Institut Polytechnique de Paris.
